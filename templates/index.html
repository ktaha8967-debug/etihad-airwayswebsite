🔑 How to Get a Google App Password

  1. Open your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Enable 2-Step Verification (if not already enabled).
  3. Go to App Passwords.
  4. Enter app name (e.g. Saudia Automation) and click Create.
  5. Copy the generated 16-letter password and paste it here.
  6. Click 🧪 Test Connection to verify instantly!
